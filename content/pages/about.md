Hi there, I'm Hank, a Python programmer / DevOps engineer based in Hsinchu, Taiwan.

I'm currently building AI agents that help wire up components on a no-code platform at TSMC. Some of my work also involves the factory side, where I develop mission allocation and path optimization systems for AMRs (autonomous mobile robots).

The coding journey started in 2022, when I tried to create a simple ledger chatbot. That's when I discovered how much I enjoy building things with my own hands, to solve my own problems, and eventually other people's.

Along the way, I ran into Linux distros and fell into them. Now I use Arch btw. You may have come across my project [aur-sync-vote](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/aur-sync-vote) if you use Arch too. It syncs your AUR votes with the packages you have installed, and it's been the most popular package on the AUR for a while.

My favorite new toy is a Framework 13 laptop, which is the ultimate developer laptop (for Linux users). 

When I am away from my keyboard, I cook, work out, and play video games. I am planning to try out DJing in the near future.
